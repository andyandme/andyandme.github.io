Project overview

Grassland Gunner was my first solo project, made with SFML using free art and music assets. It gave me experience building a complete game loop independently.

My contribution

  • Built the full game project apart from the external art and music assets.
  • Implemented menus, controls, rounds, upgrades, shooting, and progression.
  • Added dynamic music progression where later rounds increase the music speed and introduce lyrics.
